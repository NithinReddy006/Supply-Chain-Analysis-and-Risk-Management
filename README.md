INTRODUCTION

Supply chain management covers the movement of goods from order to delivery, and delivery performance is one of the main measures of its success. Companies handle thousands of orders across different products, shipping modes, and regions, and problems such as late deliveries and loss-making orders can quietly reduce customer satisfaction and profit. This project focuses on understanding delivery and operational risk in a supply chain.

The project will follow the stages covered in the course: data ingestion, cleaning, exploratory analysis, workflow management, and analytics. Apache Spark, a big data tool, will be used to process the large volume of order and shipment records. The aim is to find where and why risks occur, for example by shipping mode, region, and product category, and to present the findings clearly through visualisations and a dashboard.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Supply chains produce huge amounts of order and delivery data, yet risks such as delays and unprofitable orders often go unnoticed. The data is large, messy, and difficult to interpret without proper processing. This makes it hard for managers to identify the sources of risk and protect service quality and profit.
